STICK DUEL

Two stick fighters. Climb walls. Shoot. Every room is generated fresh.

Online uses a direct peer-to-peer connection (WebRTC) – no account, no server of ours. One player creates the room and shares the code; the other joins.

  1. Create the room
  2. Send the code or link to your friend
  3. Friend joins – the game starts automatically

Keyboard: P1 WASD + Space (jump) + F (shoot) · P2 Arrows + . (jump) + , (shoot) · Gamepad & touch supported · hold toward a wall to climb · R new room (host) · M mute
